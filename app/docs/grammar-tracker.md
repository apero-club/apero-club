## Not covered yet

Ideas for future lessons, roughly in order of usefulness at A2 to B1:

- Direct and indirect object pronouns together (*je le lui ai dit*), and *y*
- The subjunctive after *il faut que*, *je veux que*, emotions
- *Si* clauses: *si* + imparfait, conditional (*si j'avais…, je ferais…*)
- The plus-que-parfait in storytelling
- *Depuis*, *il y a*, *pendant* for time
- Possessive pronouns (*le mien*, *la vôtre*) and demonstratives (*celui-ci*)
- The gérondif (*en marchant*)
- Reported speech in the present and past
