# Image briefs

Prompts for the Décrire illustrations, written for the image tool. Every brief shares the style paragraph below, then describes the scene. Keep generous empty space on the left and right edges: the desktop layout crops the picture into a tall arch (about 5:6), the phone layout shows it square.

## Shared style

Flat, playful vector-style illustration with simple rounded shapes, no outlines, minimal flat shading, and a limited palette of electric ultramarine blue, hot pink and magenta, soft lilac, warm orange, cream, and small accents of dark green. Characters have big round white eyes with black pupils. The recurring blue pigeon appears somewhere, unbothered. Square format, simple and uncluttered, generous margins on the left and right. No text anywhere.

## Lesson 8: Une table au nom de Dupont

Scene: an elegant Paris restaurant on a Saturday evening, warm light from a small chandelier.

* At the best table, a couple in their thirties, dressed up for their anniversary, sit very straight and look nervously delighted. A rose in a small vase and a silver cloche on the white tablecloth.
* Beside them, Henri the maître d’ (fifties, blue suit, thin moustache) bows deeply with a grand sweep of his arm; a sommelier hovers behind him holding a bottle, and the chef peeks out of the kitchen door holding a tiny plate.
* At the entrance on the right, a woman in her sixties in a long black coat and dark glasses (the real Madame Dupont) has just walked in and raises one eyebrow.
* The blue pigeon sits calmly on the coat stand by the door.
* The couple has a mix of skin tones; drawn in the same simple style as the reference.
